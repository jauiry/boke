# UI Quality Review

Audit before editing and after implementation.

- Check hierarchy, alignment, spacing, typography, contrast, and density.
- Compare against the approved ink-scroll direction, not generic templates.
- Inspect 1440/1280 desktop and 390/375 mobile.
- Check hover, focus, active, loading, empty, error, and reduced-motion states.
- Verify navigation and login visibility.
- Check wrapping, clipping, overflow, touch targets, and keyboard use.
- Capture screenshots; do not claim parity without inspecting the render.
